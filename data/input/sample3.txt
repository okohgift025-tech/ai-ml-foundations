Artificial intelligence systems learn from experience and adapt to new inputs. Deep learning 
is a subset of machine learning based on artificial neural networks. Neural networks are 
computing systems inspired by biological neural networks in animal brains.

Python frameworks like TensorFlow and PyTorch enable building and training neural networks.
These frameworks provide high-level APIs for model development and deployment. Model training 
requires substantial computational resources and large datasets.

Natural language understanding is a challenging problem in artificial intelligence research.
Language models process text data to understand context and meaning. Advanced language models 
can generate human-like text and perform various language tasks with high accuracy.
